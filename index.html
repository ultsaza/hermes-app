Hermes

Hermes is a personal-use desktop application. It runs a local AI assistant (Hermes Agent) on its owner's own computer and lets that assistant access the owner's own Google accounts (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts) through Google's official APIs.

  • It is used only by its developer, with the developer's own Google accounts.
  • It is not offered, sold, or distributed to anyone else, and it has no hosted service or server.
  • Authorization uses Google OAuth 2.0 for desktop apps. Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
